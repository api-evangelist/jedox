Financial planning and analysis (FP&A) is a vital component of corporate Finance, responsible for budgeting, forecasting, planning, and supporting strategic decision-making. It provides insights that drive efficiency, growth, and profitability, ensuring businesses remain financially agile and competitive. FP&A professionals analyze financial data, market trends, and operational performance to help leadership make informed decisions.
